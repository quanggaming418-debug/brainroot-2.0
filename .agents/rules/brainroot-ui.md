# Brand, UX and accessibility
- Follow the sitewide Desktop-only decision in AGENTS.md and the desktop test matrix in docs/QUALITY_AND_TESTING.md.
- Follow `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/USER_FLOWS.md`, `docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md`.
- Use official Brainie/Rootie assets when provided; do not invent official brand imagery.
- No fake CTA, no inaccessible controls, keyboard navigation and focus states required.
- Implement reduced-motion; avoid scroll-jacking and heavyweight global 3D effects.
- Avoid white small text on peach/sage; use tested dark ink or approved contrast pairing.
- No forced account for public discovery/trial.
